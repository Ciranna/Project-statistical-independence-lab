These are synthetic educational datasets, not observational evidence. The examples are synthetic and do not establish real-world causal effects.

Key theory

Probability independence: P(A ∩ B) = P(A)P(B). Marginal frequencies summarize each variable; conditional frequencies describe one variable within a category of the other. Differences in a sample alone do not prove population dependence or causality.
